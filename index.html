TOLIS VPN

Encrypted connection for Windows. Version 1.2.0.

1. Open PowerShell and paste:
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TurboWarpFan/tolis-vpn/main/install.ps1 | iex
From Command Prompt instead: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -c "irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TurboWarpFan/tolis-vpn/main/install.ps1 | iex"
2. Connect:
tolis online
Press Ctrl+C or type tolis offline to disconnect.
